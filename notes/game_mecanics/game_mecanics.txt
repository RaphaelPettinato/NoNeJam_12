// Mechanics

Player mechanics:
    - Player will have 3 lives.
    - When he dies the game will restart.
    - The player will have access to the bosses when he reaches one determined quantity of points.
    - The player will have access to different weapons ['glock', 'shotgun', '.38']

Enemy mechanics:
    - Some enemies will move at the player's direction
    - Some of them will stay at one fixed position spawning other enemies
    - Type of enemies: {
            the ones that follows the player,
            the ones that do not follows the player, 
            and some of them will move at random direction while shooting at the player
